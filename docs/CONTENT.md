# Content Guide

## Hero

Headline direction:

> I turn messy product ideas into web software that ships.

Support:

> Full-stack development, AI integration and high-conversion web experiences for small teams and businesses that need a useful result — not a six-month discovery phase.

## Tone

- concise;
- technical but commercial;
- confident without inflated claims;
- specific nouns and verbs;
- no “10x”, “revolutionary”, “world-class”, “guru”, “ninja”, “rockstar” or fake scarcity.

## Proof language

Good:

- “Demonstrates architecture, browser graphics and deterministic QA.”
- “Built as a demonstration project for a construction lead funnel.”
- “Starting at USD 650; final quote depends on scope.”

Bad:

- “Trusted by leading companies” without clients.
- “Boost conversions by 300%” without data.
- “AI expert” as the only proof.

## Personal introduction

Use a compact introduction, not a biography:

> I’m Manuel Botto, a software developer from Argentina focused on React/TypeScript products, AI-enabled workflows and browser experiences. I use AI aggressively to accelerate implementation, but I treat tests, review and a working production build as the definition of done.

Do not add employer names unless Manuel explicitly decides to publish them.
